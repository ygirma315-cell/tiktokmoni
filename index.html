FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an account to start?
No. Steps 1–5 and the starter niche playbooks are open to everyone. An account is only useful if you want to save progress across devices or join the Pro waitlist.
My country isn't eligible for TikTok monetization. Can I still use this?
Yes — that's exactly what the free setup track (steps 1–4) is for: a dedicated phone, a trusted paid VPN and a fresh account set to an eligible region. Keep in mind that region workarounds may conflict with TikTok's terms of service, so proceed at your own discretion and follow your local laws.
How do I pay for Pro?
Payments are coming soon. Join the waitlist and we'll email you when checkout opens — waitlist members get a launch discount.
Do I need a big following already?
No. The course starts from zero — literally from a freshly formatted phone. Steps 1–5 take a brand-new account to its first 10k followers with a repeatable content system.
What are "new niche drops"?
Every month, Pro members get playbooks for emerging niches we've spotted early — including demand signals, content angles and monetization paths — before they get saturated.
What is the AI coach?
The sparkle button in the corner opens JO AI Coach — an assistant that answers questions about the course instantly: setup, niches, growth, monetization and pricing. It runs right on this page, free for everyone.
Is Google sign-in required?
Not at all. Google sign-in is just a convenience. You can sign up with email, or use the free track with no account whatsoever.
